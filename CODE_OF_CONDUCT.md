# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

As the maintainer of the **pitchimpact** project, I am committed to
fostering a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive environment for
everyone.

Participation in this project should be a positive and harassment-free
experience, regardless of age, gender identity or expression, sexual
orientation, ethnicity, religion, education level, nationality,
socioeconomic status, physical ability, or any other personal
characteristic.

The goal is to build an open and collaborative community where people
can contribute, learn, and grow with mutual respect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy, respect, and consideration for others  
- Being open to different perspectives and experiences  
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback in a respectful manner  
- Acknowledging mistakes, apologizing when appropriate, and learning
  from them  
- Prioritizing what benefits the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, or any form of harassment  
- Insulting, derogatory, or personal or political attacks  
- Public or private harassment of any kind  
- Sharing someone’s private information without explicit permission  
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or academic setting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this
Code of Conduct  
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any
behavior deemed inappropriate.

This may include warnings, editing or removing content, moderating
discussions,  
or, in serious cases, suspending or banning contributors.

Decisions will be made with fairness, transparency, and respect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with the
**pitchimpact** project, including:

- GitHub repositories  
- Issues and Pull Requests  
- Discussions and documentation  
- Any other communication channels related to the project

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these general guidelines when
determining consequences:

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or behavior without harmful
intent  
**Action:** A private message explaining why the behavior was
inappropriate and how to improve  
A public apology may be requested

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A single or repeated minor violation  
**Action:** A formal warning with clear expectations for future
behavior  
Temporary restrictions on interaction may be applied

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3. Temporary Suspension

**Impact:** Repeated or serious misconduct affecting the community  
**Action:** Temporary suspension from participating in project spaces  
No interaction with the project during the suspension period

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** Ongoing or severe misconduct, including harassment or
abuse  
**Action:** Permanent removal from the project and all associated spaces
